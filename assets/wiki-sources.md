# PEAK Wiki-derived tooltip data

All 131 English and Simplified Chinese default keys now use shared descriptions. Sourced raw/cooked/burnt/incinerated rules are expressed as conditions in the complete prose rather than replacing the description at each stage. Existing effect-conflict checks remain independent of prose selection; legacy custom state profiles are still supported by the renderer. In total, 118 of 131 direct item pages have been content-reviewed. Twelve items without a dedicated page have reviewed secondary sources. The prose is independently written; numeric effect amounts and durations belong in live modifier rows, while fixed use limits and deployment rules may remain in prose. Shared prose describes sourced behavior; it is not proof that every current effect has been parsed or game-verified.

Big Egg revision 11612 calls its rare result a Cooked Bird but separately excludes a "would-be uncooked bird" from cooking-level inheritance. The shared description states that the Egg inherits the stage and that the bird result has an exception; it does not infer the bird's exact inheritance rule. Cooked Bird revision 11126 describes normal pre-cooked spawning. Gold Prickleberry still has conflicting revisions; its shared description qualifies the baseline Thorns as raw and explicitly leaves cooked Thorns removal unverified.

## Review status and limits

All 67 profiles have been checked against the cited Wiki pages and the general cooking rules. Another 51 direct pages were reviewed and did not need state-specific prose. In-game validation is still pending. The Green Crispberry rule was also compared with the locally installed PEAK 2.6.b cooking implementation; the newly added profiles have not yet been confirmed in a live game session.

The Wiki states that Cooked and Well-Done food effects are identical and do not stack; cooked poisonous berries become safe, but mushroom poison cannot be cooked out; Shroomberries lose 5 Spores; Burnt food restores less Hunger; and each further Incinerated stage adds Poison. Sports Drink and several other items have exceptions, so the tooltip uses item-specific text only where a page documents the exception.

Gold Prickleberry is deliberately left out of this batch. The pinned revision 11109 has a conflict: the infobox says cooking removes Poison, while the prose and patch history say it removes Thorns. The search-indexed page text crawled last month now shows no cooked Poison bonus and says cooking removes Thorns, matching patch 1.40.a. Direct page and API requests return 403, so the current revision ID cannot be recorded yet. Keep its cooked-state claim withheld until a current revision can be pinned and PEAK 2.6.b behavior is checked. All 12 secondary-source items were reviewed against their cited sections and recorded revisions. Green Clusterberry is documented only on the unused-content page, so no cooking behavior is inferred from other Clusterberries.
